What the product adds

MeshInspector is the manufacturing control layer.

Measure

Dimensions, ring measurements, material weight prediction, and viewport metrics in millimeters.

Correct

Repair, resize, hollow, thicken, smooth, scoop, and prepare the generated mesh for downstream manufacturing.

Control

Version history, compare overlays, manufacturability snapshots, and current-version STL export.

The user moves from “this looks good” to “this is sized, weighted, checked, and ready to export.”
